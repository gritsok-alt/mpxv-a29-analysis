Column glossary
============================================================


[Summary]
  Metric / Value
      Headline figures for the whole ensemble.

[Per seed]
  seed
      Random seed passed to Boltz. Each seed is an independent sample; the models within one seed share a starting point.
  n
      Models generated by this seed.
  AP
      Models with the two-parallel/one-antiparallel arrangement.
  P
      Models with the symmetric all-parallel (C3) arrangement.
  X
      Models matching neither class.
  ptm / iptm / confidence
      Means over this seed's models.
  plddt_cc
      Mean pLDDT over the predicted coiled-coil segment.
  restraints_ok
      Models in which every imposed distance was within the bound.

[Per model]
  seed / model
      Seed, and the sample index within that seed. Boltz ranks samples by its own confidence, so model 0 is the best-ranked of its seed.
  class
      AP = two parallel chains and one antiparallel; P = all three parallel; X = neither. Assigned from the pairwise dot products of the per-chain helix axes.
  confidence
      Boltz composite confidence score for the whole model: a weighted combination of interface and per-residue terms. Range 0-1.
  ptm
      Predicted TM-score for the whole structure. Above ~0.5 indicates the overall fold is likely correct.
  iptm
      Predicted TM-score for the interfaces between chains. The relevant measure for an oligomer: it scores how the chains are arranged rather than how each is folded.
  plddt_N_terminal
      Mean pLDDT over the N-terminal region. Per-residue confidence, 0-100; above 70 is confident, below 50 is not.
  plddt_spacer
      Mean pLDDT over the spacer region.
  plddt_coiled_coil
      Mean pLDDT over the predicted coiled-coil segment.
  plddt_C_terminal
      Mean pLDDT over the C-terminal coil.
  d_*
      Measured CB-CB distance for each imposed restraint, in angstroms.
  max_restraint_dist
      Largest of the restraint distances in this model.
  restraints_ok
      True when every imposed distance is within the bound.
  violations
      Restraints exceeding the bound, with their measured distances.
  face_A_to_C / face_B_to_C
      Which heptad face each parallel chain presents to the antiparallel chain: e or g. The crystal has A presenting g and B presenting e.
  A_to_C_e / A_to_C_g / B_to_C_e / B_to_C_g
      Number of distinct e and g positions in contact across that interface. Higher counts mean a more decisive face assignment.

[Per residue]
  chain
      Chain after relabelling: C is the antiparallel chain, A is the restrained partner of C, B is the remaining chain.
  residue
      Residue number in the native numbering.
  rmsf
      Root-mean-square fluctuation in angstroms: how far this position moves across the accepted models after they are superposed on the fitting range. A measure of reproducibility, not of accuracy.
  plddt_mean / plddt_sd / plddt_min / plddt_max
      Per-residue confidence across the accepted models.
  n_models
      Models contributing to this position's statistics.
